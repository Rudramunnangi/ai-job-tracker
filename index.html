1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver career intelligence:

  • Account Credentials: Email address, unique username, and encrypted password hash.
  • Candidate Profile: Target role, full name, technical skills, and optional portfolio links (LinkedIn, GitHub).
  • Resume Content: Extracted plain text and PDF document contents uploaded to your Resume Vault.
  • Job Tracking Records: Companies, role titles, application dates, status stages, and job descriptions saved to your board.

2. Zero Data Sale Policy

We never sell, rent, or broker your personal information or resume contents to data aggregators, staffing agencies, or third-party advertisers. Your data is used solely to evaluate job compatibility and manage your application pipeline.

3. AI Model Data Privacy

Resume evaluations performed by Google Gemini API use enterprise API endpoints. Your proprietary resume texts and career history are not used to train public foundation models.

4. Data Security & Retention

Data transmitted between your browser and our servers is secured using TLS encryption. Passwords and OTP codes are protected using cryptographic hashing.

5. Right to Erasure

You can permanently delete your account, stored profile, uploaded resumes, and application board history at any time from Account settings. All associated records are removed from our active database.
